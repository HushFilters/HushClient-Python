nWebbed Hushfilters

Check credentials against breach data, locally.

Hushfilters uses compact Bloom filters to check whether a username and password combination may appear in known breach data. This client downloads the filters from nWebbed and checks them on your own infrastructure.

The Credential Check page hashes credentials in your browser before sending the digest to this client. Raw credentials entered on that page stay in your browser.

A match indicates possible exposure: Bloom filters can produce false positives. Results depend on the filters you have loaded, so keep them up to date.
